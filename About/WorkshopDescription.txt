The Shattered Crown turns RimWorld into a medieval party RPG. You do not build a colony. You ride out with a handful of companions, take work from the Wayfarers' Guild, meet new NPCs, complete quests, and get loot.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]What this mod is[/h1]

[b]A party, not a colony.[/b] You start alone on the road. Companions are found and recruited. Each one is a written character with their own voice, their own opinion of you, and their own reasons for coming along.

[b]Conversations that matter.[/b] Hand-written dialogue scenes. Talk a raider captain out of a fight, lie to a village elder, ask a scholar what he is not telling you. Options gated behind skill checks show their odds up front.

[img]{{IMG_DIALOGUE}}[/img]

[b]Dice-based proficiency checks.[/b] A d10 system across eleven skills. Rolls happen in conversation, at locked chests, at shrines, at rotted bridges, and at anything else the world puts in your way.

Difficulty classes rise as your party does, so a check never stops being a gamble.

[b]Eleven classes, forty-one spells and abilities.[/b] Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Warden, Wizard. Multiclass freely: every level-up offers a class level and a proficiency. Spells run on Energy, which recovers with sleep, and scale with your level in the class that granted them.

[b]Turn-based tactical combat (optional).[/b] Flip on Turn-Based Mode and fights become initiative-order, action-point tactics: move, attack, cast, end turn, with a live hit-chance readout, an AP preview on every order, and a combat log that shows the actual numbers. Toggle it off and the game plays like ordinary RimWorld.

[img]{{IMG_ACTIONPOINTS}}[/img]

[b]Dungeons with floors.[/b] Multi-level underground complexes you descend by finding the stairs: catacombs, flooded undercrofts, ossuaries, bandit-held works, hives. Each floor is generated fresh, and each one has been claimed by something different - so you learn how deep it goes by running out of stairs.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Two ways to play[/h1]

[b]The Shattered Crown[/b] - the authored campaign. A full quest chain with a written story.

[b]Wayfarers' Guild: Adventure Mode[/b] - the sandbox. No story rails. Take procedural contracts from guild halls in any friendly town, discover wilderness locations as you travel (caves, shrines, dens, bandit holes, ruined castles), hire on help, rescue captives, and hunt the crown on your own schedule.

Both modes share every system. Both are started as scenarios from the normal new-game screen.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]

[b]Requires:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url] and the Odyssey expansion (openable containers, tile mutators, underground structures).

[b]Suggested mods:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3391128917](NWN) Real Fog of War (Continued)[/url]

[b]Safe to add[/b] to an existing save, though the campaign is designed to be started from its own scenario. [b]Not safe to remove[/b] mid-save.

Works alongside most content mods. Anything that heavily rewrites melee resolution, caravan forming, or map generation may conflict.

[b]Not translated yet.[/b] English only.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]How do I level up in a specific class?[/b]
You need to find and read class books that will teach you that class.
From then on, anyone in your party can choose that class at level-up.

In [i]The Shattered Crown[/i] scenario, some companions will teach you their class.

[b]Compatible with Combat Extended?[/b]
Yes, but not thoroughly tested. Let me know if there are problems.

The mod's weapons and armor get proper CE stats (loaded only if you run CE), and turn-based mode keeps working - melee, action points, turn order, and damage scaling all function normally.
CE owns ranged ballistics and armor, so the ranged hit estimate and armor-soak readouts step aside and let CE do its job.
