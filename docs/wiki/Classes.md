# Classes and Abilities

Class levels are assigned at level-up; every class also trains three proficiencies (bonus: 1 + class level / 4 on checks). Learning a class needs its class book, or a companion who teaches it. Energy recovers with sleep. In turn-based combat, cooldowns tick one round per turn (5s): a 10s cooldown is every other turn.

## Barbarian

Fury as a fighting style. Hits harder, hurts less, and apologizes to nobody.


**Proficiencies:** Athletics, Survival, Nature

### Rage

*Level 1 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown*

For 60 seconds the caster deals +30% melee damage, feels half pain, and moves +0.3 faster, but shooting accuracy is greatly reduced.

### War Cry

*Level 2 - 15 energy, 10s cooldown, range 18.9, radius 6.9*

Not a song. Enemies within 6.9 cells break stride and swing short: -20% moving and -15% melee damage for 40 seconds.

### Unbreakable

*Level 4 - 25 energy, 41.7s cooldown*

For 40 seconds the caster feels only 10% of pain and takes 25% less damage.

### Whirlwind

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown, radius 1.9*

One full turning blow: strikes every enemy within melee range (1.9 cells) for the caster's normal weapon damage.

## Bard

A singer of the old songs: war-hymns for battle, verses for work, dirges for the enemy, and rest-songs for camp.


**Proficiencies:** Performance, Persuasion, Lore

### Battle Hymn

*Level 1 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown, range 6.9, radius 9.9*

Allies within 9.9 cells gain +0.40 move speed and +8% melee hit chance for 2 minutes.

### Inspiring Verse

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 20s cooldown, range 23.9*

One ally fights inspired for 30 seconds: melee attacks 20% harder and 15% faster, +10% shooting accuracy, and a much steadier mind.

### Dirge

*Level 3 - 15 energy, 10s cooldown, range 23.9, radius 6.9*

A song for the dead, sung early. Enemies within 6.9 cells lose their aim rather than their nerve: -15% shooting accuracy, -15% melee hit chance and -10% consciousness for 40 seconds.

### Song Of Rest

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 2 days cooldown, range 6.9, radius 9.9*

Allies within 9.9 cells gain +25% rest rate and 50% faster injury healing for 12 hours, and each restores 15 Energy. The singer gains no Energy.

## Cleric

A healer of the old faith: mends wounds, drives out sickness, and steadies the party with blessings.


**Proficiencies:** Lore, Insight, Persuasion

### Healing Touch

*Level 1 - 10 energy, 250s cooldown, range 3.9*

Heals up to 25 points of injury on the target, worst wounds first. Touch range.

### Blessing

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 1 day cooldown, range 23.9*

The target gains +10% consciousness for 1 day.

### Cleanse

*Level 4 - 10 energy, 1 day cooldown, range 3.9*

Removes diseases, wound infections, food poisoning, and toxic buildup from the target. Touch range.

### Prayer Of Mending

*Level 5 - 25 energy, 250s cooldown, radius 6.9*

Every ally within 6.9 cells of the caster mends over the next 30 seconds, closing about 12 points of injury each, worst wounds first.

## Druid

The hills' own healer. Speaks for the green things, and occasionally lets the green things speak back.


**Proficiencies:** Nature, Insight, Perception

### Nature's Balm

*Level 1 - 15 energy, 250s cooldown, range 3.9*

Heals up to 20 points of injury on the target, worst wounds first. Touch range.

### Bramble Snare

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 10s cooldown, range 23.9*

Roots the target: 85% slower movement for 30 seconds.

### Barkskin

*Level 4 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown, range 6.9, radius 0.5*

Sets one ally's skin like old oak: +40% sharp armor and +25% blunt armor for 2 minutes. One tree at a time, and all the stronger for it.

### Summon Bear

*Level 6 - 30 energy, 150s cooldown, range 4.9*

Calls a grizzly bear out of the green dark to fight at the druid's side for 60 seconds. The bear attacks nearby enemies on its own and pads back to the druid when the field is quiet; when the magic fades it vanishes without a trace. Only one summoned bear can exist at a time.

## Monk

Discipline made flesh. The monk's weapon is his fists, kept sharp by stillness.


**Proficiencies:** Athletics, Insight, Perception

### Flurry

*Level 1 - 10 energy, 20s cooldown*

For 40 seconds the caster's melee attacks come 40% faster and melee dodge chance rises by 10.

### Inner Calm

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 250s cooldown*

The monk turns it inward. Purges disease, infection, poisoning and toxic buildup from the monk alone, and leaves them composed: +10% consciousness and a far steadier mind for 60 seconds. Recovers in a quarter of the time a cleric's cleanse takes.

### Stunning Palm

*Level 4 - 15 energy, 125s cooldown, range 1.9*

Drops the touched target senseless with psychic shock. Does not work on animals.

### Serpent Stance

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown*

For 30 seconds the caster is much harder to hit in melee (+18 dodge chance) and moves +0.3 faster. The monk's armor is not being there.

## Paladin

A defender of justice. Heals the fallen, heartens the fearful, and brands the wicked for what they are.


**Proficiencies:** Athletics, Persuasion, Insight

### Lay On Hands

*Level 1 - 25 energy, 500s cooldown, range 1.9*

Heals up to 35 points of injury on the target, worst wounds first. Touch range.

### Righteous Brand

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 10s cooldown, range 23.9*

Names the wicked for what they are: the branded target's attacks falter, with sharply reduced melee hit chance and shooting accuracy for 60 seconds.

### Aura Of Courage

*Level 4 - 15 energy, 1 day cooldown, range 6.9, radius 9.9*

Allies within 9.9 cells gain -12% mental break threshold and +5% consciousness for 12 hours.

### Smite

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 15s cooldown, range 1.9*

A hammerblow of holy fire: 20 burn damage to one adjacent enemy with 50% armor penetration.

## Ranger

The road's own child: tracker, archer, and expert of the wilds.


**Proficiencies:** Nature, Survival, Perception

### Charged Shot

*Level 1 - 10 energy, 20s cooldown*

Every draw measured, every loose meant: the caster's next 3 ranged attacks fly truer (improved accuracy) and deal 150% damage (charges last up to 60 seconds; a missed shot still spends its charge).

### Hunter's Mark

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 10s cooldown, range 28.9*

The ranger settles on a quarry and does not lose it. The marked target takes +18% damage from all sources and moves 15% slower for a full four minutes. It is not a burst; it is a hunt.

### Pass Without Trace

*Level 3 - 15 energy, 500s cooldown*

The whole party moves like woodsmen for 10 minutes: enemies notice them at 40% less distance while sneaking. Stacks with darkness and light gear. Does not itself hide anyone - the party must still be sneaking.

### Swift Quiver

*Level 4 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown*

For 60 seconds the caster's ranged attacks come 35% faster and shooting accuracy rises by 4.

## Rogue

Locks, shadows, and knives. Rogues go where they aren't invited and leave with what isn't theirs.


**Proficiencies:** Thievery, Investigation, Perception

### Ambush

*Level 1 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown, range 1.9*

A single perfect strike from close quarters: deals 300% of the caster's normal weapon damage to an adjacent target.

### Shadowstep

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 10s cooldown, range 19.9*

Teleports the caster to a visible cell within 19.9 cells.

### Marked For Death

*Level 3 - 10 energy, 10s cooldown, range 23.9*

The rogue picks the one who dies first. The marked target takes +45% damage from all sources, but only for 40 seconds: a window to be used, not a debuff to leave running.

### Vanish

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 41.7s cooldown*

The caster becomes invisible for 15 seconds.

## Sorcerer

Magic as a temperament. Where the wizard studies, the sorcerer simply burns - and points the burning at whatever needs to stop existing.


**Proficiencies:** Arcana, Persuasion, Insight

### Scorch

*Level 1 - 10 energy, 5s cooldown, range 23.9*

Deals 14 burn damage to a single target with 30% armor penetration.

### Arc Lightning

*Level 2 - 15 energy, 10s cooldown, range 23.9, radius 3.9*

Deals 10 burn damage with 50% armor penetration to every enemy within 3.9 cells of the strike point. Allies are unharmed.

### Firestorm

*Level 4 - 25 energy, 41.7s cooldown, range 23.9, radius 3.5*

Sets off an explosion at the target point: 20 flame damage in a 3.5-cell radius, igniting fires. Damages allies and enemies alike.

### Frost Nova

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 20s cooldown, radius 4.9*

A ring of killing cold bursts from the caster: 10 frost damage to every enemy within 4.9 cells, and the survivors are chilled to half speed for 20 seconds. Allies are unharmed.

## Warden

A soldier of the old watch: holds the line, shrugs off wounds, and dares the enemy to come and try.


**Proficiencies:** Athletics, Perception, Survival

### Charge

*Level 1 - 10 energy, 20s cooldown*

The caster sprints at five times normal move speed for 5 seconds. Close the line before the line closes you. In turn-based combat the surge grants +4 AP on cast, offsetting most of its cost.

### Stand Fast

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 20s cooldown, range 6.9, radius 6.9*

Allies within 6.9 cells take 35% less damage for 40 seconds.

### Second Wind

*Level 3 - 15 energy, 500s cooldown*

Heals up to 15 points of the caster's own injuries, worst first.

### Challenge

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 10s cooldown, range 18.9, radius 6.9*

The warden's capstone dare: enemies within 6.9 cells of the target point suffer -15% consciousness, -15% moving, and sharply reduced melee hit chance and shooting accuracy for 40 seconds.

## Wizard

A student of the old sigils. What the ignorant call magic, the wizard calls unfinished homework.


**Proficiencies:** Arcana, Lore, Investigation

### Magic Missile

*Level 1 - 10 energy, 10s cooldown, range 23.9*

A bolt of raw sigil-force. Deals 8 burn damage plus 2 per wizard level to a single target (30% armor penetration): 10 at wizard 1, 18 at wizard 5.

### Arcane Ward

*Level 2 - 10 energy, 180s cooldown, range 23.9*

The warded target takes no damage from the next three attacks. Periodic harm such as fire burns through the ward. Lasts up to 30 seconds.

### Leaden Curse

*Level 3 - 10 energy, 10s cooldown, range 23.9*

The target moves at half speed and loses 20% manipulation for 45 seconds.

### Blink

*Level 5 - 15 energy, 10s cooldown, range 27.9*

Teleports the caster to a visible cell within 27.9 cells. The caster is briefly stunned on arrival.

## Crown Shard Powers

Granted by CARRYING a shard, not by any class. The cooldown belongs to the shard itself: hand it to another carrier and the clock travels with it. No energy cost.

### Command

*3 days cooldown, range 23.9*

Speak to an enemy in the voice the crown was made for, and for sixty seconds they are yours: they turn, and they fight the people they came with. When the voice fades they remember whose side they were on, and they are not grateful. The shard allows this once in three days; carrying it is the only price.

### The King's Mercy

*15 days cooldown, range 3.9*

Lay the shard against a living body and the worst thing wrong with it is made right: a lost limb regrown, a disease lifted, an old scar mended. Whatever the dead king could not do for his kingdom, his crown can still do for one person at a time. The shard allows this once in fifteen days; carrying it is the only price.

### Quickening

*1 day cooldown*

The whole party moves at double speed and strikes a third faster for 20 seconds, on every floor of the location. The shard allows this once a day; carrying it is the only price.

### Shardfall

*1 day cooldown, range 34.9, radius 4.5*

Call down a rain of ice shards on an area. Heavy damage to everything beneath it, friend or foe, and snow after. The shard allows this once a day; carrying it is the only price.
