# The Shattered Crown - Design Document

## Concept

A medieval-themed epic quest chain for RimWorld 1.6.
Players hunt for the lost regalia of an ancient king.

## Story Arc - "The Shattered Crown"

> Long before the ships fell from the sky, this world had its own kings. The
> last of them, Aldruin the Binder, was broken in battle and his crown
> shattered - its shards scattered to keep his power from lesser hands.

### Scenario: The Shattered Crown

The Wayfarers' Guild has been searching for pieces of the shattered crown
for years. Their search finally got a hit - a clue that led a party of
adventurers to a mountain pass, in hopes of finding a piece of the crown
or more leads to track it down.

Unfortunately, that adventuring party has been gone too long, and has not
sent a communiqué for weeks.

This is where you come in - locate their camp, and find the party, whether
they're alive or dead.
You start as a lone pawn with a horse, some crude gear, and basic supplies.


### Extensibility (post-v1)
- Side contracts from the guild (repeatable dungeon-delve quests).
- DLC integrations: Ideology relic hooks, Anomaly-flavored optional chapter.
- A guild faction presence (wandering adventurer visitors).
