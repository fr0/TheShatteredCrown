### Quest 2 - The Sunken Cellars
- **Hook:** The map marks a village lost to war. Its cellars connect into
  catacombs - and something nests there now.
- **Location:** *The Sunken Cellars* - ruined village surface; dense
  catacomb-style dungeon of narrow rooms; insectoid/vermin threats.
- **Objective:** Clear the catacombs, recover the **Kingsblade** (an excellent-
  quality longsword) and the first crown shard clue.
- **Reward:** The Kingsblade + loot.
- **Chain:** Triggers Quest 3.

### Quest 3 - The Bandit Baron
- **Hook:** The royal signet needed to open the king's barrow is held by the
  Iron Brand - a bandit company squatting in a fortified keep.
- **Location:** *Ironbrand Keep* - walled keep with courtyard, barracks, and
  a defended great hall. Heaviest combat chapter.
- **Objective:** Assault the keep, defeat the Baron, take the **Royal Signet**.
- **Reward:** Signet + weapons/armor loot from the bandit armory.
- **Chain:** Triggers Quest 4.

### Quest 4 - The Silent Monastery
- **Hook:** The guild's scholars need the location of the barrow. The archive
  of a plague-abandoned monastery holds the last copy of the royal chronicle.
- **Location:** *The Silent Monastery* - intact but eerie; cloister, chapel,
  scriptorium. Light on combat, heavy on atmosphere; traps and a few
  surprises guard the archive.
- **Objective:** Retrieve the **Royal Chronicle** from the scriptorium.
- **Reward:** Medical/scholarly loot (medicine, drugs, art).
- **Chain:** Triggers Quest 5.

### Quest 5 - The Barrow of the First King (Finale)
- **Hook:** Everything points here: the barrow of Aldruin the Binder. The
  signet opens the door. What guards the crown has waited a long time.
- **Location:** *The King's Barrow* - multi-chamber tomb dungeon ending in a
  throne room with an elite guardian retinue ("the Honor Guard": high-quality
  plate/melee pawns) around the king's sarcophagus.
- **Objective:** Fight through the barrow, defeat the Honor Guard, claim the
  **Shattered Crown**.
- **Reward:** The **Shattered Crown** - a unique legendary apparel artifact
  (strong social/psychic bonuses), plus gold and royal treasure.
- **Chain:** Ends the arc; epilogue letter from the guild.
