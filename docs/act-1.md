# Act 1 - The Wayfarers' Call

1. Locate the adventuring party. The initial quest points to Bran's location.
2. The player finds Bran in a ruin. He points the player to the party's campsite; he also optionally joins the party.
3. The player goes to the campsite and meets Bran's party members.

Bran's party reveals that they have been stalking an Ettersnap for months.
Ettersnaps are very rare creatures that are known to be able to extract
memories from the dead, and there are rituals that can bring those memories
to light. Bran and his company believe this particular Ettersnap may have
memories that will lead them to the shard.

Unfortunately, Ettersnaps are very elusive and difficult to catch. This one
has proven especially so.

Bran's group has some requests for the player, which layer in as optional
sidequests:

1. Track down some supplies. Ettersnaps can be lured using moss. The group
   knows there are probably some nearby. The player can get a quest to go
   pick them up (naturally, there are threats to deal with).
2. Deal with some local raiders. Bran's crew has been playing a cat-and-mouse
   game with them. Eliminate them.
3. Send a message to they guild. Bran's crew has been incommunicado and would
   like to send a message, but they lost their last raven. They know of a local
   druid who might be able to help. (This druid is also a recruitable companion).

And then the main quest:

The crew has located the Ettersnap, in a cave nearby (different world map location,
the cave is a sub-map). Go there and acquire the Ettersnap, and bring it back.
If the player did quest #1, this will be slightly easier.
